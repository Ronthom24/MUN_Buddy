# MUN Buddy - Project Overview

## 1. Introduction

MUN Buddy is a comprehensive Model United Nations (MUN) conference management platform designed to digitize and simplify every stage of organizing and participating in MUN conferences.

Traditional MUN conferences rely on multiple disconnected tools such as Google Forms, Excel sheets, WhatsApp groups, emails, PDFs, and manual assignments. This often leads to administrative overhead, data duplication, communication gaps, and inefficient workflows.

MUN Buddy aims to solve these challenges by providing a centralized, scalable, and production-grade platform that manages the complete lifecycle of a conference—from its creation by organizers to participation by delegates and post-conference activities such as certificates and feedback.

The platform is designed to support multiple conferences simultaneously while maintaining data isolation, security, and scalability.

---

# 2. Vision

To become a complete digital ecosystem for Model United Nations conferences by providing organizers, staff, and delegates with an intuitive, secure, and highly automated conference management platform.

The long-term vision is to eliminate repetitive administrative work while improving the conference experience for everyone involved.

---

# 3. Objectives

The primary objectives of MUN Buddy are:

- Simplify conference management.
- Eliminate manual data handling.
- Automate delegate registration and approvals.
- Organize committees and portfolio assignments.
- Provide secure document and resource management.
- Improve communication between organizers and delegates.
- Maintain accurate conference records.
- Build a scalable architecture capable of supporting conferences of all sizes.

# 4. Target Users

The system serves three primary user groups.

## 4.1 Organizers

Organizers have complete administrative control over conferences.

Responsibilities include:

- Creating conferences
- Managing conference settings
- Creating committees
- Creating portfolios
- Reviewing registrations
- Assigning delegates
- Uploading conference resources
- Publishing announcements
- Managing staff members
- Monitoring conference progress
- Generating certificates
- Viewing analytics

---

## 4.2 Staff Members

Staff members assist organizers with conference management.

Depending on permissions, staff can:

- Review delegate registrations
- Manage committees
- Assign portfolios
- Upload documents
- Send announcements
- Moderate discussions
- View conference data

Permission levels may vary depending on organizer settings.

---

## 4.3 Delegates

Delegates use the platform throughout the conference lifecycle.

Delegates can:

- Create an account
- Register for conferences
- Submit required information
- Upload documents
- Track application status
- Receive committee and portfolio assignments
- Download study guides
- Access conference resources
- Receive announcements
- View schedules
- Obtain certificates

# 6. System Workflow

The typical conference lifecycle is as follows:

1. Organizer creates an account.
2. Organizer creates a conference.
3. Organizer configures conference settings.
4. Organizer creates committees.
5. Organizer creates portfolios.
6. Registration opens.
7. Delegates register.
8. Organizers review applications.
9. Delegates are approved or rejected.
10. Approved delegates receive committee assignments.
11. Organizers assign portfolios.
12. Resources become available.
13. Announcements are published.
14. Conference is conducted.
15. Certificates are generated.
16. Conference data is archived.

# 8. Architecture

The backend follows a layered architecture.

Presentation Layer

↓

Routes

↓

Controllers

↓

Services

↓

Database Models / Queries

↓

MySQL Database

Business logic remains inside services, while controllers only coordinate requests and responses.

This separation improves maintainability, testing, and scalability.
