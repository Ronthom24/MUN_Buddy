Executive Summary

Emerging technologies are transforming international security faster than traditional legal and diplomatic frameworks can adapt. Artificial intelligence, cyber capabilities, autonomous weapons systems and advanced surveillance technologies are reshaping military strategy, national security planning and geopolitical competition.

Governments face a complex challenge: encouraging innovation while preventing destabilizing uses of technology. International organizations increasingly debate how emerging technologies should be regulated, monitored and integrated into global governance structures.

For the Security Council, the issue centers on how technological developments affect international peace and security and what actions states can take collectively to reduce risks.

Key Issues

Cyber operations have become a major component of modern conflict. States increasingly use cyber capabilities for intelligence gathering, infrastructure disruption and strategic influence.

The international community continues to debate norms governing cyber conduct and state responsibility in cyberspace.

Artificial intelligence has applications across defense, surveillance, logistics and intelligence.

While offering significant benefits, AI also raises concerns regarding accountability, misuse and unintended consequences.

Autonomous systems capable of selecting and engaging targets create significant ethical and legal debates.

Many governments advocate increased regulation while others prioritize technological flexibility.

Governments increasingly employ advanced surveillance technologies for security purposes.

Balancing security requirements with privacy and civil liberties remains a major challenge.

Energy grids, telecommunications networks and financial systems increasingly depend on digital infrastructure.

Protecting these systems from cyber threats is a growing international priority.

India's Position

India views emerging technologies as critical drivers of economic development, national security and strategic competitiveness.

The country supports innovation and technological advancement while emphasizing responsible use, sovereign decision-making and international cooperation.

India generally advocates balanced regulatory frameworks that promote innovation without creating unnecessary restrictions on technological growth.

In discussions related to artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and emerging technologies, India frequently highlights capacity-building, technology access and equitable participation for developing nations.
