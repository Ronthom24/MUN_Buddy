Why This Committee Matters

Global Influence

The Security Council is the most influential body within the United Nations regarding international peace and security.

Its decisions can directly affect governments, conflicts and international relations.

Binding Authority

Unlike most UN bodies, many Security Council resolutions are legally binding.

This makes UNSC debates significantly more impactful than discussions in most other committees.

Diplomatic Significance

The UNSC serves as a platform where major powers negotiate, compete and cooperate on international crises.

Powers & Limitations

What UNSC Can Do

  • Pass binding resolutions
  • Impose sanctions
  • Authorize peacekeeping missions
  • Authorize military intervention
  • Establish investigative bodies
  • Respond to international crises
  • Recommend admission of new UN members

What UNSC Cannot Do

  • Ignore the UN Charter
  • Act without required voting procedures
  • Solve purely domestic issues without international implications
  • Bypass veto realities
  • Enforce unrealistic resolutions lacking implementation mechanisms

Membership Structure

The Permanent Five hold unique influence within the Security Council through their veto power.

  • United States 🇺🇸
  • United Kingdom 🇬🇧
  • France 🇫🇷
  • Russia 🇷🇺
  • China 🇨🇳

Any substantive resolution can be blocked by a veto from a permanent member. Understanding the priorities and red lines of P5 nations is critical for successful diplomacy.

Non-permanent members serve two-year terms and possess full voting rights but do not have veto power.

These states often play a decisive role in coalition-building and can significantly influence negotiations.

Voting & Decision Making

Procedural Votes

Procedural matters concern how the committee conducts its work.

  • Require 9 affirmative votes
  • No veto applies
  • Used for agenda setting and procedural motions

Substantive Votes

Substantive matters involve resolutions and decisions affecting international peace and security.

  • Require 9 affirmative votes
  • Cannot pass if vetoed by a P5 member
  • Most important votes in committee

Rules of Procedure

A motion proposes an action for the committee. Examples include opening speakers lists, entering caucuses and moving into voting procedures.

Points are used to address procedural concerns, seek clarification or raise issues affecting committee operations.

A resolution is the committee's proposed solution to an agenda item. It outlines actions, recommendations and implementation mechanisms.

Resolution Lifecycle

1. Research & Negotiation

Delegates identify issues and build alliances.

2. Working Paper

Initial ideas are discussed and refined.

3. Draft Resolution

Ideas are formalized into official clauses.

4. Sponsors & Signatories

Support is gathered from delegates.

5. Debate & Amendments

Clauses are debated and revised.

6. Voting

Committee adopts or rejects the resolution.

Committee Strategy Guide

How To Succeed In UNSC

  • Understand P5 dynamics
  • Build alliances early
  • Negotiate before formal debate
  • Know your country's red lines
  • Focus on realistic solutions

Common Delegate Mistakes

  • Ignoring veto considerations
  • Speaking without policy knowledge
  • Overusing procedural motions
  • Drafting unrealistic clauses
  • Failing to negotiate

Reality Check

  • UNSC can impose sanctions.
  • UNSC can authorize peacekeeping operations.
  • UNSC can respond to armed conflicts.
  • UNSC cannot magically solve every international issue.
  • UNSC resolutions must remain realistic and implementable.
  • Political realities often shape outcomes as much as legal authority.

Committee Approach To Current Agenda

Emerging Technologies & International Security

The Security Council approaches emerging technologies through the lens of international peace and security.

Key concerns include:

  • Cyber warfare
  • Artificial intelligence in military systems
  • Autonomous weapons
  • Critical infrastructure protection
  • International technology governance

Delegates should focus on actionable and enforceable solutions that fall within the UNSC's mandate.
