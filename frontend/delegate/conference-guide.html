Message From The Secretariat

Welcome to RITMUN 2026. RITMUN aims to create an environment where diplomacy, critical thinking, negotiation and leadership intersect. This conference is designed not merely as an academic exercise but as a realistic simulation of international decision-making.

Delegates are encouraged to move beyond speeches and focus on collaboration, coalition-building, research-driven debate and practical policymaking.

Success at RITMUN is not determined solely by how often you speak, but by how effectively you influence discussion, build consensus and contribute meaningful solutions.

Conference Rules & Expectations

Delegates are expected to maintain professional standards throughout the conference.

Western formals are required during all committee sessions, ceremonies and official conference activities.

Professional appearance contributes to the diplomatic atmosphere of committee and reflects the seriousness expected of participants.

Delegates are expected to attend all committee sessions.

Repeated absences may affect eligibility for awards and reduce opportunities for participation.

Consistent attendance demonstrates commitment and professionalism.

Electronic devices may be used only in accordance with committee guidelines.

Delegates should prioritize active participation over screen time and remain engaged throughout proceedings.

How UNSC Operates at RITMUN

Debate Structure

Committee sessions typically alternate between formal debate, moderated caucuses and unmoderated caucuses.

Delegates are encouraged to actively engage in both structured and informal negotiations.

Resolution Development

Ideas progress from discussion to working papers and eventually draft resolutions.

Strong resolutions are collaborative, realistic and aligned with committee mandate.

Speaking Time

Speaking times may vary depending on committee progress and chair discretion.

Delegates should communicate clearly, concisely and with policy accuracy.

Participation Expectations

Quality of participation matters more than quantity.

Constructive contributions, diplomacy and leadership are valued throughout committee.

A Note From Experienced Chairs

Many delegates believe awards are won through speeches alone. In reality, some of the strongest delegates spend most of their time negotiating, drafting solutions, building alliances and helping move committee forward.

Leadership is often demonstrated through collaboration and influence rather than speaking frequency.

Delegate Survival Guide

First MUN?

Many first-time delegates worry about not knowing enough, speaking poorly or making mistakes. The reality is that preparation and participation matter far more than perfection.

Focus on understanding your country, your committee and your agenda. Confidence develops throughout committee.

What If I Freeze During A Speech?

Pause. Take a breath. Return to your country's position. Committee values substance over flawless delivery.

What If I Get Challenged?

Defend policy, not emotion. Strong delegates remain calm, cite facts and return discussion to the agenda.

What If I Don't Know Procedure?

Observe experienced delegates, ask chairs for clarification and focus on understanding the purpose behind motions and points.

Advanced Delegate Guide

Bloc Building

Identify countries with aligned interests early and begin informal discussions before major debates begin.

Negotiation

Most committee outcomes are determined outside formal speeches through strategic negotiations.

Resolution Leadership

Contributing ideas and coordinating drafting efforts often creates stronger committee influence.

Diplomatic Influence

Influence comes from trust, preparation and consistency rather than volume.
