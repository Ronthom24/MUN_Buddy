Economic Intelligence

India is one of the world's fastest-growing major economies and is currently among the largest economies globally. Its economic model combines strong domestic consumption, expanding manufacturing capabilities, a rapidly growing digital economy, and an increasingly influential services sector.

The government continues to prioritize infrastructure development, industrial expansion, technological innovation, and global investment attraction through initiatives such as Make in India and Digital India.

  • Information Technology & Software Services
  • Pharmaceuticals & Biotechnology
  • Manufacturing & Heavy Industry
  • Telecommunications
  • Agriculture & Food Processing
  • Financial Services
  • Renewable Energy
  • Automobile Production

India maintains extensive trade relationships with the United States, China, UAE, European Union, Saudi Arabia, Japan and Singapore.

Trade priorities focus on technological cooperation, energy security, supply-chain resilience and export growth.

Challenges include employment generation, infrastructure gaps, regional inequalities and energy dependence.

Opportunities include digital transformation, AI adoption, semiconductor manufacturing, renewable energy leadership and expanding global influence.

Military Intelligence

India maintains one of the world's largest standing armies.

Primary responsibilities include border security, territorial defence, counter-terrorism operations and rapid-response deployment.

Major focus areas include the northern borders, modernization efforts and indigenous defence production.

The Indian Navy safeguards maritime trade routes and projects power throughout the Indian Ocean Region.

Strategic objectives include maritime security, anti-piracy operations and maintaining regional stability.

The Air Force provides air superiority, strategic deterrence, surveillance and rapid deployment capabilities.

India is a recognized nuclear weapons state with a declared No First Use policy and maintains a credible minimum deterrence doctrine.

Foreign Policy Doctrine

Strategic Autonomy

India traditionally pursues strategic autonomy, seeking to maintain independent decision-making rather than formally aligning with major power blocs.

This principle explains India's unique voting behaviour, diplomatic flexibility and balancing of relationships with competing global powers.

Regional Priorities

India prioritizes stability and cooperation in South Asia while maintaining influence throughout the Indian Ocean Region.

Relations with neighbouring countries remain a central pillar of national strategy.

Global Priorities

India seeks a greater role in global governance, UN reform, sustainable development, technology cooperation and international security.

A permanent seat on the Security Council remains a long-term objective.

Multilateral Engagement

India actively participates in BRICS, G20, QUAD, SCO, G77 and numerous UN bodies.

Multilateral diplomacy is considered a major instrument of Indian foreign policy.

Strategic Partnerships

India maintains strategic partnerships with the United States, France, Japan, Australia, Russia, UAE and several European and Asian powers.

These relationships focus on defence cooperation, technology transfer, trade, energy security and regional stability.

National Interests

National Security

India's primary security concerns include border disputes, cross-border terrorism, maritime security, cybersecurity threats and regional instability.

The government prioritizes territorial integrity, counter-terrorism operations and strategic deterrence.

Economic Growth

Maintaining high economic growth remains one of India's most important national objectives.

Infrastructure expansion, manufacturing growth, foreign investment and technological development are key priorities.

Trade & Connectivity

India seeks diversified trade partnerships, resilient supply chains and greater participation in global commerce.

Energy imports, export competitiveness and economic resilience remain critical concerns.

Technology & Innovation

India views technology as a strategic asset.

Artificial Intelligence, semiconductors, space technology, cybersecurity and digital governance have become major priorities.

Climate & Sustainability

India balances climate commitments with developmental requirements.

Renewable energy expansion, energy security and climate financing remain central priorities in international negotiations.

Allies & Strategic Partnerships

Relationship: Strategic Partnership

India and the United States cooperate extensively in defence, technology, trade and regional security.

Areas of disagreement occasionally emerge on trade, human rights discussions and geopolitical approaches.

Russia remains one of India's oldest strategic partners.

Defence cooperation, energy security and historical ties continue to shape relations.

India often balances its relationship with Russia while expanding ties with Western nations.

France is one of India's most reliable strategic partners.

Cooperation includes defence, space technology, nuclear energy and Indo-Pacific security.

International Blocs & Organizations

What is it? The G20 is a forum of the world's largest economies.

India's Role: India is a founding member and hosted the 2023 G20 Summit.

Why It Matters: Provides influence over global economic governance, development priorities and climate discussions.

BRICS brings together major emerging economies.

India promotes multipolarity, economic cooperation and Global South representation through BRICS.

Members: India, USA, Japan, Australia.

Focus: Indo-Pacific security, technology cooperation, maritime stability and disaster response.

Issue-Based Voting History

Russia–Ukraine Conflict

India has generally adopted a position of strategic neutrality and frequently abstained on resolutions directly condemning Russia.

Reasoning includes historical ties, defence cooperation and strategic autonomy.

Israel–Palestine

India traditionally supports a two-state solution while maintaining strong bilateral relations with Israel.

Voting behaviour often reflects this balancing approach.

Climate Change

India advocates climate justice and differentiated responsibilities.

Developed nations are expected to take greater responsibility for historical emissions.

Counter-Terrorism

India consistently supports strong international action against terrorism and cross-border extremism.

This remains one of India's most consistent foreign policy positions.

Current Affairs Briefing

Stay aware of developments that may arise during debates, press conferences, points of information and informal negotiations.

India–China Border Developments

Border management and military deployments along the Line of Actual Control continue to remain an important strategic concern for India.

India–US Strategic Cooperation

Technology transfer, defence partnerships and Indo-Pacific cooperation continue to deepen.

Semiconductor & Technology Initiatives

India is actively investing in semiconductor manufacturing and emerging technologies to reduce strategic dependence.

Renewable Energy Expansion

India continues to pursue ambitious renewable energy goals while balancing developmental requirements.

Defence Modernization

Significant investment continues in indigenous defence production and modernization of military capabilities.

Global South Leadership

India increasingly positions itself as a leading voice for developing nations in international forums.
