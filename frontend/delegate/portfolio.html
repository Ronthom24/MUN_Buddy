Portfolio Overview

The Permanent Representative serves as the official voice of the nation within the United Nations system. The role involves representing government policy, negotiating with other member states and advancing national interests within international forums.

Delegates assigned this portfolio must think strategically, communicate diplomatically and maintain consistency with national objectives while engaging constructively with the international community.

Unlike domestic political leaders, Permanent Representatives operate primarily through diplomacy, negotiation and coalition-building rather than direct executive authority.

Diplomatic Mission

Policy Execution

The Permanent Representative operates as the direct diplomatic link between the Government of India and the United Nations. While strategic direction originates from national leadership, the Representative is responsible for translating policy into diplomatic action.

Within committee, delegates should think of themselves as senior diplomats entrusted with protecting national interests, building international partnerships and communicating government priorities.

Core Objectives

Protect National Interests

Ensure all negotiations and policy proposals align with India's strategic, economic and security priorities.

Promote International Cooperation

Work constructively with other states to develop practical and consensus-based solutions.

Strengthen Global Standing

Advance India's position as a responsible and influential international actor.

Maintain Policy Consistency

Ensure statements, negotiations and votes remain consistent with official government positions.

Policy Red Lines

Every portfolio has positions it cannot realistically compromise on. Understanding these boundaries is critical to maintaining credibility and policy consistency throughout committee.

National Sovereignty

Oppose measures that unnecessarily undermine sovereign decision-making or territorial integrity.

National Security

Protect strategic interests and oppose initiatives that create disproportionate security risks.

Policy Consistency

Avoid supporting proposals that directly contradict established national policy positions.
